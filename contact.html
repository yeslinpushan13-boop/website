FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order Zymomax probiotic feed?

You can order directly through our Products page. Add items to your cart and checkout securely online. For bulk orders, contact us at orders@zymomax.com.

Do you offer fermentation consultancy for non-aquaculture sectors?

Yes! While our flagship products are for aquaculture, our fermentation consultancy and scale-up advisory covers food fermentation, biogas, biopharmaceuticals, and more.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For online orders, there's no minimum. For bulk discounts, MOQ is typically 50 kg for feed products and 10 kg for supplements. Contact us for custom pricing.

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery within Tamil Nadu and Karnataka: 3–5 business days. Pan-India express: 5–7 business days. Cold chain shipping is available on request.

Can you develop a custom probiotic formulation for my farm?

Absolutely. Our R&D team provides custom formulation services tailored to your species, local conditions, and performance goals. Contact us for a detailed consultation.
