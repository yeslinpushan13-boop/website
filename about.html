Who We Are

Pioneering Aquatic Biotech from India

Zymomax Biotech is a science-driven startup born out of a passion for fermentation and a commitment to sustainable aquaculture. Founded and incubated at DETI@ACE-TBI, Adhiyamaan College of Engineering, Dr MGR Nagar, Hosur, we bridge the gap between cutting-edge fermentation science and real-world aquaculture challenges.

We specialize in developing, manufacturing, and commercializing probiotic feeds for aquatic animals — particularly shrimp — while also providing comprehensive fermentation consultancy and scale-up advisory services to biotech companies.

Our incubation at ACE-TBI gives us access to world-class infrastructure, expert mentorship, and a thriving innovation ecosystem that powers everything we do.

Our Guiding Principles

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To revolutionize Indian aquaculture by delivering scientifically validated probiotic solutions that enhance shrimp health, reduce antibiotic dependency, and improve farmer profitability — sustainably.

🔭

Our Vision

To become India's leading aquatic probiotic company and a globally recognized center of excellence in fermentation science, serving aquaculture farms across Asia and beyond by 2030.

💡

Our Values

Science before marketing. Farmer welfare first. Transparency in research. Sustainability at every step. We believe aquaculture and ecology can thrive together.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2022
Concept & Research Initiation
Research began on indigenous probiotic strains for aquaculture applications at Adhiyamaan College of Engineering's biotechnology laboratory.
2023
DETI@ACE-TBI Incubation
Selected and incubated by DETI@ACE-TBI. First pilot-scale probiotic formulation successfully developed and validated in shrimp trials.
2024
Company Registration & Product Launch
Zymomax Biotech formally incorporated. ZymoShrimp Pro™ launched commercially. First fermentation consultancy contract signed.
2025 →
Scale-Up & National Expansion
Expanding product line, establishing distribution partnerships across aquaculture states (TN, AP, Odisha, Gujarat). Scale-up to 500L bioreactor capacity.
